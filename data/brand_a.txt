TechGadgets - Comprehensive Policy Document

1. Return Policy:
Customers may initiate a return for any reason within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be in original packaging. We process refunds within 5-7 business days upon receiving the return.

2. Refund Policy:
Full refunds are provided for defective items or items returned within the 30-day window. For returns beyond 30 days, we do not offer refunds.

3. Shipping Policy:
We ship via FedEx Ground. Orders above $50 qualify for free shipping. Standard shipping is $7 flat rate. International shipping is calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.

4. Broken or Damaged Items:
If an item arrives broken, we offer an immediate replacement. Please contact support immediately, and no need to send the broken unit back to us. We prioritize your convenience.

5. Warranty Information:
All electronics come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. This does not cover water damage or accidental drops.

6. International Returns:
International customers are responsible for return shipping costs. Returns must be authorized by our support team first.
