HomeComfort - Furniture and Decor Policy

1. Return Policy:
Furniture returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery. Must be in original condition. A 15% restocking fee applies to all furniture returns.

2. Damaged Items:
If furniture is damaged during shipping, our white-glove delivery team will handle the return and replacement at no extra cost.

3. Shipping:
Flat rate shipping of $50 for all furniture. Accessories ship for free on orders over $30.

4. Warranty:
5-year limited warranty on all wooden structures. Does not cover upholstery or finishes.
