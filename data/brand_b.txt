FashionFlow - Fashion & Apparel Policy

1. Return Policy:
All returns must be initiated within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn and with tags attached. Please use the portal on our website to generate a return shipping label.

2. Refund Policy:
We provide store credit only. No cash refunds. Store credit never expires and can be used on any item in our shop, including sale items.

3. Shipping Policy:
We ship via standard courier. Flat fee of $10 for all orders under $100. Orders above $100 get free shipping.

4. Broken or Damaged Items:
If an item is damaged upon arrival, please send a photo to support@fashionflow.com. We will inspect it and issue store credit equivalent to the item's purchase price.

5. Sizing & Care:
Please consult our sizing chart before ordering. Due to the delicate fabrics we use, we recommend professional dry cleaning for most items.
